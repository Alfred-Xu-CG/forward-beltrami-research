# Route L — flat-torus zero mode and periodic hard decoder

## 1. Periodic geometry

On the unit flat torus, opposite sides are identified. A map is represented by
a periodic correction plus an affine period lift. Let `h=F_barz` and
`m=mean(h)`. The zero-mode-safe formula is

`F(z)=z+m conjugate(z)+C(h-m)`.

The two periods are

`p_x=1+m`, `p_y=i(1-m)`.

The mean cannot be discarded: it controls the affine deformation of the
fundamental parallelogram.

## 2. Positive graph embedding

The torus experiments use positive-weight graph equations on periodic
connectivity. Diagonal and extended directions add representational freedom for
cross-direction shear while positive weights preserve local orientation in the
tested lifted triangulation.

## 3. VJP

For the affine map `F(z)=z+mu conjugate(z)`, a map cotangent `g` contributes

`dL=Re(sum conj(g) conjugate(z) dmu)`,

so the real-inner-product VJP is proportional to `sum g z`. Period cotangents
add the explicit derivative of `p_x` and `p_y`.

## 4. Scope

This route is a controlled periodic test of zero modes, periods, and graph
expressivity. It is not evidence for bounded-domain boundary closure or a
general triangulated-surface hard layer.
