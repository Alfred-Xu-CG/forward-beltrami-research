**Current interpretation.** The retained image-to-latent network computes a 257×257-vertex fixed-diagonal P1 map (131,072 triangles), with exact positive saved binary32 Q1 corner signs and ordered boundary on the audited outputs. It improves mean manual-landmark TRE against the *same* initial affine and frozen safe start on seven previously viewed pairs and on all twenty stain directions of one previously viewed lung specimen. The full learned map also beats a vertex-L2-fitted affine correction of its frozen start in all twenty directions, so the measured benefit is not just another affine correction under that comparator. Those twenty directions are highly correlated, not twenty new patients. No untouched patient-group anatomy evaluation or matched strong-baseline comparison has yet established competitive, generalizable registration. The external image matcher and RANSAC affine are outside the differentiable layer. The safe geometry and development-specimen local improvement are real engineering results; the general application claim remains unproved.

All new experiment logs, model checkpoints, maps and full per-case JSON reports live on **D:** under `D:\QC_optimization_data\digital_topology_wsi\ACROBAT_train_subset\scaleup102\imageonly_extension`.
