# Multiscale residual-innovation proposals through one safe P1/Q1 vertex table

## Predeclared research card

**Question.** The five-level absolute Gaussian target \(v+D_{\sigma_l}(v)\) worsened a matched-width synthetic control and all three sparse-pathology development pairs. Is the failure caused partly by narrow-band levels overwriting a useful coarse map with a poorly supported target? Can successive *residual-innovation* proposals preserve the coarse solution while adding warranted fine detail, with the same hard topology layer and feasible forward/VJP memory?

**Exact construction.** Let \(U_l\) be the safe vertex map after refinement to level \(l\). For accepted correspondences \(q_m\mapsto p_m\), evaluate the current Q1 map at \(q_m\) and define residual displacement \(e_{lm}=p_m-U_l(q_m)\). With the same separable Gaussian weights and denominator \(+10^{-8}\), define \(R_l(v)=\sum_m w_{lm}(v)e_{lm}/(\sum_m w_{lm}(v)+10^{-8})\). Propose **\(U_l(v)+R_l(v)\)**, pass it through the unchanged four-color F1-D per-vertex safety layer, and refine dyadically before the next level. Use the already frozen widths \((.12,.06,.03,.015,.01)\) at \(17²,33²,65²,129²,257²\). At the identity initial state, the first pass reduces to the previous Gaussian motion proposal, up to floating-point evaluation. At later levels, if all matches are already represented, \(R_l\) vanishes. In unsupported regions, the \(+10^{-8}\) denominator attenuates corrections rather than overwriting \(U_l\) toward identity. This is a sequence of additive *proposals to one vertex table*, not composition/resampling of five homeomorphisms.

**Guarantee and limitations.** The Gaussian residual field itself has no injectivity theorem. Every F1-D pass keeps all affected Q1 corner signs positive in exact arithmetic under its existing hypotheses; dyadic refinement exactly represents the previous Q1 map in exact arithmetic; ordered identity boundary remains fixed. Fixed SW–NE P1 interpretation is positive because its two face signs are among those Q1 signs. Saved float32 archives require a new exact-binary certificate. The chain is almost-everywhere differentiable in match coordinates/displacements and F1 latents; Q1 cell selection and active safety constraints introduce nonsmooth boundaries. It is not an image-to-latent learned network, universal approximation theorem, or standalone registration result.

**Falsifier and decisive test.** Unit-test zero residual, nonzero bounded correction, exact Q1 signs, and finite source/target VJP. Then use the *same* analytic sinusoidal target, 16² match centers, all three update schedules (constant-width 65² baseline, matched `(.12,.06,.03)` 65² baseline, five-level) and the same 10,000 seeded off-grid queries as `H_MULTI_BANDWIDTH.md`; compare absolute versus residual innovation at identical widths. Test the five-level residual version on all three already saved SuperGlue development match sets with no retuning, recording held-back machine-match error, Q1/P1 original-JPEG TRE, saved signs, decoder-only forward/VJP and allocated peak. A true innovation benefit requires a paired same-width comparison, not merely outperforming the old constant-width baseline. Do not choose widths using anatomy labels.

**Prior work / novelty boundary.** Coarse-to-fine residual correction is a standard multiscale idea. This particular network-compatible decoder combines a differentiable sparse-motion residual, exact same-grid refinement, and hard F1-D feasibility; it is an implementation hypothesis rather than a new generic theorem or a claim that sparse matches reveal fine anatomy.

## Independent residual-innovation review

The coordinates and direction are consistent. Machine matches `q→p` both live in the fixed normalized frame after forming `J=I_M∘A`; the desired residual correspondence is `U(q)=p`. The code's temporary target `q+(p-U(q))` makes the Gaussian helper's subtraction recover exactly the intended innovation `p-U(q)` in real arithmetic. Kernels remain centered on the original domain coordinates `q`, corrections are added to current values `U(v)`, and the output affine is applied once afterward. There is no hidden composition, second affine inversion, anatomical-label input or detached residual path. A zero innovation is a fixed point, but this does not prove monotonic match-error reduction after interpolation, clipping and safety scaling.

Independent analytic-target and four-weight Q1 calculations give residual constant/matched/five-level vertex RMSE **.009886415989/.003995380555/.002553858901** and off-grid RMSE **.009978348024/.004045181195/.002583923716**. All three residual synthetic maps and all **twelve real matched-width maps** have **262,144 positive exact integer corner signs each**, exact identity boundaries, and unchanged positive-rational affine factors (identity for synthetic). The real reports agree on the source/target match files, seed-290929 split and appropriate three/five width lists.

Separate affine inversion followed by SciPy bilinear solves or independent 2×2 triangle solves reproduces every Q1/P1 landmark entry in the four-way table, with all **77/77, 78/78, 69/69** roots unique and maximum per-label discrepancy **2.63e-12 px**. In particular residual coarse→five-level Q1/P1 means are **28.58702765/28.58287623 → 33.44030889/33.41980999** (HistoReg), **9.04340309/9.04328322 → 9.25924660/9.25939464** (lesion), and **7.78995174/7.78989122 → 7.91630191/7.91621888** (kidney). Thus the paired real deterioration and the opposite synthetic result are both supported. Held-back machine-match RMSEs also reproduce by direct saved-map interpolation.

Two fixtures delimit the residual and gradient claims. Already-fitted nonidentity matches produce exactly zero correction; incorrectly reusing original `p-q` instead produces an unwanted **.01580** correction. For a Q1 knot with neighboring x-slopes **.8/1.2**, autograd returns the selected **1.2** branch although the classical two-sided derivative does not exist. Synthetic 16² half-grid matches become grid vertices at finer levels, so a finite source-coordinate VJP there is not a unique-Jacobian proof. Away from knots, a separate 65² residual-mode source/target directional derivative agrees with central differences within **2.22e-9**. Tests: **10 passed, 1 CUDA-only skipped**.

The evidence supports failure of these specific sparse-match/width protocols, not a causal diagnosis that sparsity or noise alone caused it; neither was independently varied. It motivates investigating conditional fine-level use but does not validate a label-free selection rule. P1 is a separately evaluated interpretation of maps whose innovations queried Q1, not a P1-trained objective. Decoder timings exclude the external proposal chain and are not end-to-end neural inference. No implementation edits or remote GPU jobs were performed for this review.
