# Feature-matcher scale-subset ablation

## Predeclared research card

**Question.** DHR's cached SuperPoint/SuperGlue initializer runs two rotations × six registration sizes (12 network calls) per image pair. Can a fixed, smaller scale subset materially reduce initial-stage latency while keeping useful image-only global alignment on the three already inspected cross-stain development pairs?

**Exact claim under test.** With the same cached eval model, weights, preprocessing, angle search, image files, four CPU threads and GPU device, the two subsets below need 6 and 4 calls respectively rather than 12. Whether they preserve field topology and landmark target registration error (TRE) is an empirical question, not a theorem.

**Assumptions.** DHR's `multi_feature` enumerates the `registration_sizes` list inside each candidate angle and selects the candidate with the most matches. Changing that list alters the initializer and may select a different global affine. The saved initial-only field remains a strong external image proposal, not a learned image-to-Q1 network. All three labels are already inspected development data and enter only an independent evaluator after registration.

**What would falsify utility.** Major TRE deterioration, missing inverse landmark roots, orientation/topology failure, or negligible initial-stage latency improvement relative to the 12-call cached control. A faster but inaccurate setting is not useful.

**Smallest decisive test.** Add a wrapper-only CLI override for registration sizes, with a unit test that the default stays unchanged and invalid/empty lists fail. Fixed subsets, chosen before new runs and without reading landmark scores: `S6=[150,250,400]` (two rotations × three sizes = six calls) and `S4=[200,350]` (two × two = four). Run each on BIRL lesion and kidney first; if executable and output-comparable, run HistoReg too. Measure full wall, DHR initial-stage, actual matcher calls/model builds, field shape, exact saved-field four-corner signs and independent TRE. Compare within pair to the cached 12-call control. Full HistoReg wall time includes its previously measured ~37-s image-loading stage, so initial-stage latency is the relevant matcher comparison. Do not tune subsets after seeing development landmarks; any later adaptive-size search needs a new protocol. Keep all persistent fields/weights/logs on D: and small reports in Git.

**Prior work / novelty boundary.** Multiscale feature matching and candidate pruning are standard engineering. This ablation only asks whether a smaller fixed search could make *this* reusable DHR-based H-mode initializer more practical; it makes no novel neural-layer or topology claim.

## Execution and results

The wrapper added `--registration-sizes` with an explicit validation test; omitting it leaves the DHR configuration unchanged. All six candidate runs used the isolated DHR 1.0.1 GPU environment on the AI host, four CPU threads, an idle RTX A6000 GPU0 or GPU2, the same JPEGs, the same image-only **initial-only** pipeline, and the previously checked in-process eval-model cache. `S6` specified `--registration-sizes 150 250 400` and `S4` specified `--registration-sizes 200 350`; `angle_step=180` remained unchanged. BIRL JPEGs used loading ratio 1, while HistoReg used .1, matching their respective cached-12 controls. The commands never accepted landmark CSVs. Each complete DHR result folder, including the MHA field, actual config and logs, is stored at `D:\QC_optimization_data\digital_topology_wsi\dhr_gpu_s{6,4}_{histo,lesions,kidney}`. Small runtime and separately computed evaluator JSONs are versioned beside this report. Times are single observations of pipeline construction, registration and saving inside a new process, not complete CLI-process times or distributions; the OS file cache may already have been warm.

The independent saved-field evaluator inverted the same original-JPEG landmark coordinates through each field, requiring a unique in-cell Q1 inverse root per label, and checked every cell's four Q1 corner determinants. TRE is Euclidean distance in **fixed JPEG pixel coordinates**; a mean is defined only when all corresponding moving labels have a unique inverse. Kidney uses the declared 69-ID intersection, excluding two fixed-only IDs. No labels influenced registration or the preselected subsets.

Actual matcher calls were 12, 6 and 4, respectively, with **one** model construction/load per candidate command. Read-back of the six saved subset fields gave **0 nonpositive Q1 cells** on all four corner checks (738,540 HistoReg; 653,260 lesion; 869,778 kidney cells per run). Unique inverse coverage was **77/77, 78/78 and 69/69** for both subsets. This is an empirical check on six particular DHR outputs, not a guarantee for all inputs or a full global-boundary proof. SimpleITK readback found the `S6` MHA vector field **bitwise identical** to the cached-12 field in all three pairs (same shape, `numpy.array_equal=True`, maximum difference zero). Therefore the useful initializer selected on these examples was among the six `S6` candidates; it does **not** show that excluded scales are universally dispensable. `S4` selected different fields, modestly improving kidney's development-label mean but worsening HistoReg and lesion. These differences were observed *after* subset selection and must not be used to retune the declared subsets then claim held-out accuracy.

The cached-12→S6 initial-stage reductions are approximately **15%, 26%, 27%** for HistoReg/lesion/kidney; S4 gives about **37%, 32%, 44%** but changes the field. HistoReg's cold total barely moves because the separate DHR loading log spends about 37 s before feature matching. With only three inspected development pairs, no repeated randomized timings and no independent cross-stain test groups, this is an engineering candidate-pruning observation rather than a broadly validated speed/accuracy frontier. The strongest safe Q1 result remains a separate geometry representation; these DHR fields are *not* inherently guaranteed homeomorphic, and neither subset makes the image-to-Q1 network a learned, fast registration frontend. A useful next H-mode experiment would initialize the safe residual with the selected image-only affine, then compare nonrigid image-based refinement under a shared budget, while counting the external matcher cost.

## Independent saved-output review

Readback independently confirms **bitwise S6/cached-12 field equality in all three cases**. Comparing complete configs within each pair finds only the scale list, case name and logging path changed; runtime files report 6/4 calls and one model build, with nonrigid fitting disabled. Installed DHR enumerates angles `-180,0`, then the supplied sizes, and picks the first strictly best match count. The CLI default preserves the existing list and its four focused tests pass. Identical outputs are evidence for these three inputs, not a proof that the same scales suffice elsewhere.

Without calling the production evaluator, I enclosed all four determinants of every saved S6/S4 cell using outward-rounded float64 interval operations on the exact stored binary32 displacement plus integer grid coordinates. All lower endpoints are strictly positive. Minimum lower bounds for HistoReg/lesion/kidney are **`.9474552991/1.0043570702/.9060802661`** (S6) and **`.9501771867/.9987562547/.9104061872`** (S4), over the reported **738,540/653,260/869,778** cells. This establishes exact local signs of these stored Q1 fields, not a global boundary certificate.

For S4, independent convex-quadrilateral candidate selection followed by SciPy bilinear inversion recovers **77/77, 78/78, 69/69** unique roots and mean TRE **`46.6751373452982/8.2213767329701/10.3228767593857`** fixed-JPEG pixels. No failures were dropped. The maximum discrepancy from any reported landmark TRE is `1.82e-12`. The independently implemented coordinate chain retains half-pixel centers, padding and kidney's `787/768` initial-resampling ratio; kidney's two fixed-only labels remain outside the declared intersection. Two deliberate attacks behaved correctly: an x-reflection produced 24 negative corner bounds on a 3×4 grid, while treating the backward translation `(1/4,-1/8)` as its inverse introduced `.559017` coordinate error.

Timing scope clarification: table “full” means pipeline construction plus registration and final saving, **not the complete CLI process**; imports, argument parsing and config writing precede its timer. Initial-stage timing includes the first model construction, candidate processing and transform selection, whereas the separately reported inference timer includes prediction-to-CPU conversion but excludes some setup. One construction indicates a cold process-local model cache, not a cold OS disk cache. Configs store generic `cuda`, not physical GPU identity; reported A6000 GPU0/GPU2 runs and single observations support an exploratory comparison, not matched-device repeated latency statistics. The percentage reductions match the saved runtimes. No GPU jobs or production edits were used for this review.
