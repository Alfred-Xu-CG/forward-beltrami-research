## Terms and limits

BIRL's GitHub repository reports BSD-3-Clause for its software. The [ANHIR dataset terms](https://anhir.grand-challenge.org/Data/) state **CC-BY-NC-SA** for challenge data, require source acknowledgement and citations, and require a Grand Challenge account plus joining the challenge for the full download. Treat these public sample images/landmarks conservatively under the ANHIR noncommercial/share-alike research conditions; do not infer that the BIRL code license overrides image rights. The [ANHIR FAQ](https://anhir.grand-challenge.org/FAQ/) says only part of all landmarks is public and full dataset access remains challenge-gated.

The author example HistoReg CD68→CD4 pair on D: is a third development example, but its patient/block/set identity is unavailable and may not be assigned a new independent group without authoritative metadata. The local MIIT-v4 archive also lacks a verified source→target manifest and specimen identifiers (`EVIDENCE.md`). HyReCo's [IEEE DataPort record](https://ieee-dataport.org/documents/hyreco-hybrid-re-stained-and-consecutive-histological-serial-sections) still needs record-specific access, license, package size, and pair/group metadata verification before download. ACROBAT's [official data page](https://acrobat.grand-challenge.org/data/) lists archives hundreds of gigabytes in size and hidden target landmarks for validation/test; it is not a small next-pair source.

## Can the public maintainer repositories supply ten groups?

No verified ten-group subset is available from their public GitHub files. The BIRL Git tree has exactly the two real histology groups above; its other sample JPEGs are artificial transforms of one reference image. The maintainer's separate [dataset-histology-landmarks repository](https://github.com/Borda/dataset-histology-landmarks) has five 5%-scale JPEGs for `lung-lesion_3`, but its committed landmark files for that set are user annotations at 50% scale, rather than corresponding 5%-scale consensus CSVs. The repository documents a generation script, yet the coordinate conversion and generated labels have not been checked here, so this is one *candidate* extra group, not a third ready paired-landmark group. The full [ANHIR collection](https://anhir.grand-challenge.org/Data/) has over 50 sets with matched landmarks, but the official [access FAQ](https://anhir.grand-challenge.org/FAQ/) requires account registration and joining the challenge. That is the practical route to ten or more set-level groups; no account-gated download was attempted.

**Practical next step:** run image-only registrations on both BIRL pairs at their supplied 5% scale, then evaluate the saved maps against their corresponding CSVs in a separate evaluator. Use the two sets as distinct development cases and disclose that they do not supply a train/validation/test split. For formal G2/G3, obtain the full ANHIR training package through the official account/join route, retain all images/scales of each set together, inspect available patient/block metadata, and reserve entire sets before training or tuning.

## Follow-up scout: ten or more real groups (2026-09-29)

The best *registration* candidate remains [full ANHIR](https://anhir.grand-challenge.org/Data/): its official page says **50+ histological sets**, each comprising differently stained consecutive slices with matched ImageJ `X,Y` landmark CSVs at 5%, 10%, 25%, 50%, and 100% scales. Set directory is a mandatory grouping key; all scales and stain combinations of a set must stay in one split. It is legitimately available for research under **CC-BY-NC-SA** after Grand Challenge registration and joining the challenge; download was **not** attempted, so archive bytes, current account access, and the number of usable public-label sets remain unverified. Importantly, 50+ *sets* do **not** establish 50+ independent animals/patients: the official description says, for example, three mouse lung lesions, four mouse lung lobes from the same histological samples, and two mammary-gland blocks. For a leakage-safe patient/animal/block split, inspect the complete set provenance after access and merge related sets before partitioning. Published challenge test landmarks may be withheld/server-only; use only released training landmarks, strictly in evaluation.

[HyReCo's peer-reviewed dataset paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10704256/) reports **81 pairs and about 3,000 manual landmarks**. Subset A has nine consecutive-section stacks, including H&E, CD8, CD45RO, Ki67 and re-stained PHH3; subset B has 54 additional H&E→PHH3 re-stained pairs (see the [paper PDF](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1842563/FULLTEXT01.pdf)). Its [IEEE DataPort record](https://doi.org/10.21227/pzj5-bs61) is described as openly available by the authors, but this scout could not inspect the record's file list, bytes, license, or download behavior (IEEE page blocked automated access). The nine stacks are below a ten-stack threshold; whether subset B represents 54 independent specimens/patients is unverified. Thus HyReCo is a plausible manual-access follow-up, **not a verified leakage-safe ≥10-group subset**.

Two other open sources meet the *pair count* but **not** the requested independent deformation-label criterion. [Petríková et al.'s 77-patient H&E/Ki67 collection](https://zenodo.org/records/11218961) has explicit patient-pair grouping, but the distributed pairs are **already rigidly aligned** and lack independent correspondence labels; its 39 ZIPs total **197 GB**, each about 2.9–7.0 GB. The [article](https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5729/9/8/100) states CC-BY-4.0, whereas the Zenodo record's visible license field is blank, so confirm rights at acquisition. The [206 BF/SHG TMA-core pairs](https://zenodo.org/records/4550300) are also pre-aligned (2048²; 1.8 GB total, 57 MB smallest ZIP), without a verified patient/block grouping or independent deformable landmarks. Neither should be presented as a real registration accuracy benchmark. [HER2match](https://zenodo.org/records/15797050) supplies 17 H&E/HER2 WSI pairs and 21,172 aligned tiles, but no independent landmark set is listed; its files total 44.9 GB and the record shows copyright rather than a clear reuse license.

**Decision:** no immediately downloadable, verified ≥10 *independent specimen* groups with raw paired images, evaluator-only correspondences, clear grouping/terms, and small practical size were established in this bounded scout. Full ANHIR is the actionable acquisition route for ≥10 **set-level** registration cases, conditional on account access and a provenance audit; HyReCo is second priority if its DataPort package and grouping can be inspected. Do not train, register, or select hyperparameters using evaluation landmarks.
